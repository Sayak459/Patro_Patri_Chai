Why Patro
Patri Chai?

Plenty of people can plan a beautiful wedding, and plenty of platforms can introduce two strangers. That's not really the question. The question is what happens when those things are held by the same people who already understand relationships, understand Bengali culture, and understand how to tell a story well.

We're not trying to win you over with a clever new concept. The goal is simpler and, we think, harder: if PPC is doing it, you already know it will be beautiful, organised, personal, and properly executed.

  • An audience that already trusts how we tell stories
  • A real understanding of Bengali culture and ritual
  • A creative team that thinks in scenes, not just checklists
  • One point of contact, start to finish
