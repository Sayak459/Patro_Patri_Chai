What is
Patro Patri Chai?

Patro Patri Chai began as a story — a piece of content built around the very Bengali business of finding someone. The idea underneath it was always bigger than one season of television.

Patro Patri Chai is the story. PPC is the ecosystem it belongs to — a growing set of services around relationships and weddings, connected by the same instinct for care, culture and craft.

Some people will meet us through the show. Others will meet us because they're planning a wedding and have never watched a single episode. Both are exactly where they should be — nothing here is a forced funnel from one to the other. A match doesn't owe us a wedding. Content doesn't owe us a client. Each part stands on its own, and together they simply make each other stronger.
