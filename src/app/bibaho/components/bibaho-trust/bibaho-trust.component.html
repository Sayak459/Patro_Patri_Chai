We don't want PPC to be an interesting idea people forget.

The goal isn't novelty. It's the moment someone plans a Bengali wedding and thinks — obviously, PPC does this.

How we grow

i.

One wedding, done exceptionally

We'd rather execute a handful of weddings brilliantly than take on more than we can hold to our own standard.

ii.

Every wedding teaches the next

What we learn becomes the discipline behind the next one — on purpose, not by accident.

iii.

We grow at the pace our standards allow

Scale is a result, not a goal. We'd rather be trusted at a small size than known at a careless one.
